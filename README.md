# An Evening Room — Acrylic Star

Books stacked on a desk. Curtains swaying by the window. A light that briefly glows warmer just before fading out.

A familiar scene in what could be anyone’s room.

But what felt ordinary a moment ago may not stay that way.

## How to Play

Walk up to anything that catches your eye and click to examine it.
Move books and a smartphone, open desk drawers, and look inside furniture. Some things cannot be discovered just by looking.

**Finding something does not always mean you should keep moving forward. Remembering familiar places—and the way you came—may prove useful.**

A familiar space, taking an unfamiliar shape.

There is no need to rush. Take your time exploring the light, materials, and subtle changes around you.

## Controls

Playing on a PC with a keyboard and mouse is recommended.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / A / S / D | Move relative to the direction you are facing |
| Hold B while using WASD | Sprint |
| Drag on the screen | Look around |
| Click an object | Examine, move, open, or close it |
| Buttons at the bottom of the screen | Switch to viewpoints near the desk, bed, and other locations |
| “Under the Bed” button | Crouch and crawl under the bed |
| Drag while examining the pouch | Rotate the pouch |
| Scroll or pinch while examining the pouch | Zoom in or out |
| “Back to Room” / Esc | Leave the inspection view |
| Click / Enter / Space during dialogue | Advance to the next line |

Note: You cannot sprint under the bed.  
Note: Use WASD to move through the hallway; the viewpoint buttons are only available inside rooms.  
Note: Dialogue also advances automatically.

## Things to Explore

- **Books and smartphone:** Move them to check underneath.
- **Desk drawer:** Click the printed document inside to read it in full.
- **Closet:** Open the doors and place clothes and hangers on the floor, one at a time.
- **Light switch:** Turn the room’s main light on or off.
- **Desk lamp:** Turn it on or off independently of the main light.
- **Under the bed:** Explore from a low viewpoint to find things normally hidden from sight.
- **Doors:** Open them and step outside the room.

Hover over an interactive object to see a description at the bottom of the screen.

## About the Game

### Play with the Light

The light comes on quickly, but fades out with a brief, warm afterglow.
Changing the lighting gives the same room a slightly different character.

### Rooms That Change as You Explore

Beyond the hallway lie more rooms, each with a different furniture arrangement.
Sometimes, **the arrangement is not the only thing that has changed**.

During the same playthrough, returning to a room preserves its layout and unusual features.
Continuing forward will not automatically loop you back to the first room.

### Stay Until the End

Beyond the exploration, rolling credits and a short dialogue epilogue await.

As you read, take a moment to look around the room on screen, too.

## Getting Started

1. [Play here](https://wakana-code.github.io/The-Frontroom-English-/)
2. Open the link in your browser while connected to the internet.

An internet connection is required at startup because external libraries, including Three.js, are loaded from a CDN.

If you are running a downloaded copy and opening the file directly does not work, try serving it through a local web server or a static-site hosting service.

### Recommended Environment

- An up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, or a similar browser
- WebGL support
- A keyboard and mouse
- Browser hardware acceleration enabled

Rendering shadows, water reflections, and transparent materials can be demanding. Performance may vary depending on your device.

## Please Note

- There is no save feature. Reloading the page starts a new game.
- The game contains unsettling imagery and surreal room layouts.
- Walking and sprinting cause gentle camera movement. Near the end, there are also simulated video noise and brief visual distortions. If these effects cause discomfort, please take a break.
